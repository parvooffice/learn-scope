# 2-minute demo script

Record with any screen recorder (1440×900, browser zoom 100%). Chrome or Edge so the GPU badge says **WebGPU**.

| Time      | Do                                                                                                                                                                                          | Say                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Open the landing page. The spiral hero animation runs. Click **Try it**.                                                                                                                    | "This is LearnScope: you can watch a neural network learn, live, in the browser, running on the GPU."                                        |
| 0:15–0:40 | Playground trains the spiral. Point at the decision boundary, the network graph, the loss curve.                                                                                            | "Weights are red or blue lines, the heat map is what the network believes, and these charts show gradients and activations for every layer." |
| 0:40–1:05 | Drag the learning-rate slider to ~1.5. Wait ~8 s for the **Model Doctor** cards (it reports _Learning rate looks too high_ plus its consequences: saturated units and vanishing gradients). | "Normally you only see a loss number. The Doctor reads the telemetry and tells me the learning rate is too high, with the evidence."         |
| 1:05–1:20 | Click **Show me** on the learning-rate card, then **Lower the learning rate to 0.01 and restart**. Training restarts and the Doctor says "No problems detected".                            | "It jumps to the step where it went wrong, and one click applies the fix."                                                                   |
| 1:20–1:35 | Drag the **timeline** back through training; boundary, weights and histograms rewind. Click Live.                                                                                           | "Every step is recorded, so I can time-travel and inspect the whole network at any moment."                                                  |
| 1:35–1:50 | Open **Backprop**; press Next through the steps to the update.                                                                                                                              | "This explains backprop with real numbers from my from-scratch autograd engine, checked against numerical gradients."                        |
| 1:50–2:00 | Open **MNIST**, train 5 seconds, draw a digit, show prediction and feature maps.                                                                                                            | "And it trains a CNN on real handwritten digits on the GPU; draw a digit and watch it flow through the network."                             |

Suggested GIF: the 0:15–1:35 stretch, 12 fps, ~900 px wide (use a tool like ScreenToGif).
